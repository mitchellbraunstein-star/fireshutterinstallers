Questions

Frequently Asked

How often should fire shutters be serviced?

Most fire shutters should be inspected and drop-tested regularly — typically at least annually, and more often in high-use environments. We'll recommend a schedule based on your specific installation and provide the documentation you need for compliance.

Do you offer a free site survey?

Yes. Every quote starts with a free, no-obligation site survey so we can measure accurately, check access, and recommend the right system for your premises.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Toronto and the entire Greater Toronto Area — including Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill, Scarborough, North York, and Etobicoke — plus wider Ontario including Oakville, Burlington, Hamilton, and Durham Region.

What types of buildings do you work with?

We install fire shutters across commercial, retail, industrial, and warehouse premises throughout Toronto and the GTA — from single shop fronts to large industrial openings.

How quickly can you respond to an emergency?

We offer 24/7 emergency callout across the GTA for failed or damaged shutters to keep your site secure and minimize disruption.

Will my shutters meet current fire safety standards?

We supply tested, fire-rated systems and provide full test and handover documentation so you can demonstrate compliance to inspectors and insurers.
